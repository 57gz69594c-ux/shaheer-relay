ISSUE #1: GAS FUNDING POLICY DIGEST MISMATCH (BLOCKS 4 CHAINS)
================================================================

STATUS: Fixed for ETH, POL, AVAX, LINEA. BROKEN for ARB, BASE, OP, BNB.

The gas_funding.py file computes a per-chain policy_digest() using:
  hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(policy, sort_keys=True, separators=(',',':')).encode()).hexdigest()

Where "policy" is the per-chain dict from load_policy(chain_id).
This digest is stored in gas_funding_policies SQLite table.

validate_funding_policy() loads the policy, computes digest, compares to DB.
If mismatch: "ValueError: Funding policy is absent or differs from its immutable journal"

WHAT WENT WRONG:
- Stored SHA256 of the FULL policy file (all chains) in the DB digest column
- Engine computes SHA256 of just the PER-CHAIN dict
- DB digest and computed digest are different

FIX NEEDED:
For chains 42161 (ARB), 8453 (BASE), 10 (OP), 56 (BNB):
1. Load per-chain policy with load_policy(chain_id)
2. Compute policy_digest(per_chain_dict)
3. Store that digest in gas_funding_policies.digest
4. Store json.dumps(per_chain_dict, sort_keys=True) in policy_json column
5. Ensure _initial_evidence() validation passes

CONSTRAINT: Engine source files are SHA256 hash-verified by verified_launcher.py.
CANNOT modify gas_funding.py, preflight.py, competitive_engine.py, etc.
Must fix data/config only.
