  STORY 2 — ARIEL SCHOOL, ZIMBABWE
═══════════════════════════════════


📍 WHERE & WHEN

Sept 16, 1994

~10:15 AM — morning recess

Ariel School, Ruwa

20 km east of Harare, Zimbabwe


Teachers inside — staff meeting

62 kids outside — unsupervised


---


🛸 WHAT THEY SAW

Silver disc — silent — drops from sky

Lands in brushy field

~100 meters from the playground

Just past the "out of bounds" line

Lasted about 15 minutes


---


👽 THE BEINGS

1 or more small figures stepped out

3 to 4 feet tall

Black tight suit

Pale waxy skin

Huge black eyes — "size of a rugby ball"

Long dark hair — "like Michael Jackson"

Didn't walk — floated


---


🧠 THE MESSAGE

Telepathic — no words spoken

Environmental warning

"Take care of the planet"

Salma (witness):
"It didn't touch me physically,
but with that stare,
it touched every ounce of my body"

"We need clean air,
beautiful plants,
clean soil to live and thrive"


---


👧 KEY WITNESSES

Emily Trim — confirmed telepathic contact

Salma — felt the stare + environmental message

Dallyn Vico — claimed on Netflix he made it up

BUT in 2008 he said:
"I was witness to an event
I cannot fully explain"


---


🔬 THE INVESTIGATION

Dr. John Mack — Harvard psychiatrist

Flew to Zimbabwe

Interviewed kids one by one

Asked them to draw what they saw

All drawings matched —
silver disc, big-eyed beings

Kids were crying, visibly shaken

62 children — same story — told independently
