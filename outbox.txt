▸ TRAILING STOP MECHANISM

  How it works:
  1. Entry: fixed stop loss at 1.5x ATR
  2. Price moves 1.5x risk in our favor: TRAILING ACTIVATES
  3. Stop follows price tightly (0.5x risk behind best price)
  4. Price keeps moving? Stop ratchets up, locking more profit
  5. Price reverses? Exit with profit already locked in

  58% of trades activated trailing stop.
  Avg trailing stop exit: +$40.47 per trade.
