🎬 WHO

Hollywood writer + producer

Co-created Dark Skies (NBC)

Star Trek: Enterprise

📍 WHERE & WHEN

Orcas Island, Washington State

Friend's 20-acre wooded property

Morning — friend had gone to the store

Brent was alone

🚶 THE WALK

Went for a solo walk on the property

Came over a ridge

Saw a tall blonde man

Standing ~20 feet away

Further down the hill

Longish blonde hair

European-style clothing

Exceptionally well put together

Brent is 6'4" — this being was tall


---


🧠 THE TELEPATHY

Mouth did not move

Said: "Hello Brent"

Knew his name

Told him to turn around

And walk the other way


---


👻 THE VANISHING

Brent turned around

Looked back —

The figure had vanished

Completely gone


---

Tall Whites maintain a gated compound
on the island

Arrive via unmarked black helicopters

Every couple of months

During "high energy periods"

~8 of them stay for several days

They come to recharge

Selective about who they interact with

on Earth?"

  STORY 2 — ARIEL SCHOOL, ZIMBABWE
═══════════════════════════════════


📍 WHERE & WHEN

September 16, 1994

~10:15 AM — mid-morning recess

Ariel School, Ruwa

20 km east of Harare, Zimbabwe


---


🏫 THE SETUP

Teachers were all inside

Staff meeting

62 children outside on the playground

No adult supervision at all


---


🛸 WHAT THEY SAW

Silver disc — completely silent

Descended rapidly from the sky

Landed in a brushy field

~100 meters from the playground

Just past the "out of bounds" line

The whole thing lasted about 15 minutes


---


👽 THE BEINGS

1 or more small figures stepped out

About 3 feet tall

Scrawny neck — thin limbs

Black tight-fitting suit

Pale, waxy skin

Huge black eyes —
one kid said "size of a rugby ball"

Long straight black hair —
another kid said "like Michael Jackson"

They didn't walk — they floated


---


👁️ THE STARE

When the children locked eyes
with the being —

Images and intense feelings
entered their minds

It didn't speak —
it transmitted


---


🧠 THE MESSAGE

Telepathic — no words spoken

An environmental warning

"People would be dying
if we don't change our ways"

Humanity is harming the planet


Salma (witness):

"It didn't touch me physically,
but with that stare,
it touched every ounce of my body"

"We need clean air,
beautiful plants,
clean soil to live and thrive"


---


👧 KEY WITNESSES

Emily Trim — spoke publicly years later
Confirmed telepathic contact

Salma — described the intense stare
Received the environmental message

Dallyn Vico — on Netflix claimed
he made it all up

BUT — in a 2008 interview he said:
"I was witness to an event
I cannot fully explain"

His own words contradict his denial


---


🔬 DR. JOHN MACK — HARVARD

John E. Mack

Harvard Medical School psychiatrist

Pulitzer Prize winning author

Flew to Zimbabwe within days


---


📋 THE INTERVIEWS

Interviewed dozens of students
one by one — on camera

Used semi-structured interviews

Asked 35 children to draw
what they saw

All drawings matched —
silver disc, big-eyed beings

Kids were crying, visibly shaken

62 children — same story —
told independently


---


🎓 HARVARD TRIED TO STOP HIM

Mack's alien research
was controversial at Harvard

His colleagues tried to oust him

Harvard investigated him
for 14 months

But in the end —
the dean publicly stated:

"We reaffirm Dr. Mack's
academic freedom to study
what he wishes and to state
his opinions without impediment"

Mack survived the ouster attempt


---


💀 HOW JOHN MACK DIED

September 27, 2004

Age 74

Hit by a car in London

Driver: Raymond Czechowski, 52

Drunk driver

Pleaded guilty to
"careless driving under
the influence of alcohol"

  STORY 3 — MICHAEL HERRERA
  INDONESIA 2009
═══════════════════════════════════


🪖 WHO

Michael Herrera

20 years old

U.S. Marine — infantry rifleman

2nd Battalion, 5th Marines

2nd Platoon, Echo Company

6-man squad


---


🌊 THE EARTHQUAKE

September 30, 2009

7.6 magnitude earthquake
hits Sumatra, Indonesia

Triggered a tsunami

Massive devastation

Herrera's unit redirected
to Indonesia for humanitarian aid


---


🚁 THE INSERTION

October 8, 2009

Squad inserted via CH-53 helicopter

Dropped into a clearing
northeast of Padang city

West Sumatra

Mission: provide security
for aid supply drops to locals


---


⚠️ SOMETHING WAS OFF

They were NOT issued radios

Herrera has emphasized this
repeatedly — very unusual

For a mission like this
you always have comms

They were cut off from command
in dense jungle terrain


---


⛰️ THE CLIMB

Squad climbed 900 feet
up a ridge

Securing positions
to overlook the supply drop zone

Dense Indonesian jungle
all around them


---


👀 THE DISCOVERY

From the top of the ridge

Something massive
in a clearing in the jungle

Herrera pulled out
his Panasonic camera

Took pictures and video
of what he was seeing

🛸 THE CRAFT

~300 feet wide

Octagonal shape

Hovering silently
just below the treetops

Rotating clockwise

Shifting colors —
light matte gray
to dark matte black

Low bassy hum —
like a guitar amplifier
or a transformer buzzing

A separate platform
sat on the ground beneath it


---


📦 THE LOADING OPERATION

beneath the craft

The platform rose up

And merged into the UFO

They were loading
the craft with cargo


---


🚶 THE APPROACH

No radios to report what they saw

So they decided to move closer

Crept down the hill in formation

Got within 150 feet of the craft


---

Bulletproof vests

night vision attachments

elite U.S. special operations

Speaking American military jargon

on the Marines immediately


---


🚫 NO COVER

no tactical advantage

from vegetation

But concealment is NOT cover

Vegetation is not bulletproof

They were completely exposed


---

Batteries disappeared

Confiscated their rifles

Took all their ammunition

Took everything


---

Fully dressed in uniform

But NO visible name tag

That detail stuck with Herrera
