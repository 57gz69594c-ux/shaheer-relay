▸ KEY FINDINGS:
  1. RF beats Claude on direction accuracy (65.8% vs 57.1%)
  2. Claude IC is HIGHER (0.27 vs 0.12) — probability rankings have signal
  3. Brier score WORSE than baseline — calibration adds noise
  4. Placebo test: 96% agreement — context NOT being used meaningfully
  5. Repeatability: 96.7% direction consistency (excellent)
  6. Event days: 57.1% DirAcc, IC 0.53 (small n=12)
  7. When Claude disagrees with RF, Claude is right 75.8% of the time

▸ WHAT THIS MEANS:
  - Claude can't beat a Random Forest at predicting gold direction
  - BUT Claude's probability rankings correlate with actual outcomes (positive IC)
  - Claude ignores the news/context it's given (placebo test proves it)
  - Claude may be useful as an ENSEMBLE feature alongside ML models
  - Claude should NOT have independent trading authority

▸ PROMOTION GATE: INCONCLUSIVE
  Claude has IC signal but fails direction accuracy and placebo tests.
  No independent trading authority. May serve as ensemble feature.
