  STORY 1 — BRENT FRIEDMAN
  NORDIC ALIEN, ORCAS ISLAND
═══════════════════════════════════


🎬 WHO

Brent V. Friedman

Hollywood writer + producer

Co-created Dark Skies (NBC)

Star Trek: Enterprise

Star Wars credits


---


📍 WHERE & WHEN

Orcas Island, Washington State

Friend's 20-acre wooded property

Morning — friend had gone to the store

Brent was alone


---


🚶 THE WALK

Went for a solo walk on the property

Came over a ridge

Saw a tall blonde man

Standing ~20 feet away

Further down the hill


---


👤 THE BEING

Looked like a Scandinavian male model

Longish blonde hair

European-style clothing

Exceptionally well put together

Brent is 6'4" — this being was tall


---


🧠 THE TELEPATHY

Mouth did not move

Said: "Hello Brent"

Knew his name

Told him to turn around

And walk the other way


---


👻 THE VANISHING

Brent turned around

Looked back —

The figure had vanished

Completely gone


---


🏠 WHAT HIS FRIEND EXPLAINED

Tall Whites maintain a gated compound
on the island

Arrive via unmarked black helicopters

Every couple of months

During "high energy periods"

~8 of them stay for several days

They come to recharge

Selective about who they interact with


---


🍺 THE PUB STORY

One local account:

A Tall White walked into a pub

Ordered a drink

Asked the bartender:

"What is it like to live here
on Earth?"

Then left calmly


---


🎙️ WHERE HE TOLD THE STORY

Jesse Michels — American Alchemy

Episode: "Hollywood, The CIA & UFOs"

With Bryce Zabel

Clips went viral everywhere




═══════════════════════════════════
  STORY 2 — ARIEL SCHOOL, ZIMBABWE
═══════════════════════════════════


📍 WHERE & WHEN

Sept 16, 1994

~10:15 AM — morning recess

Ariel School, Ruwa

20 km east of Harare, Zimbabwe


Teachers inside — staff meeting

62 kids outside — unsupervised


---


🛸 WHAT THEY SAW

Silver disc — silent — drops from sky

Lands in brushy field

~100 meters from the playground

Just past the "out of bounds" line

Lasted about 15 minutes


---


👽 THE BEINGS

1 or more small figures stepped out

3 to 4 feet tall

Black tight suit

Pale waxy skin

Huge black eyes — "size of a rugby ball"

Long dark hair — "like Michael Jackson"

Didn't walk — floated


---


🧠 THE MESSAGE

Telepathic — no words spoken

Environmental warning

"Take care of the planet"

Salma (witness):
"It didn't touch me physically,
but with that stare,
it touched every ounce of my body"

"We need clean air,
beautiful plants,
clean soil to live and thrive"


---


👧 KEY WITNESSES

Emily Trim — confirmed telepathic contact

Salma — felt the stare + environmental message

Dallyn Vico — claimed on Netflix he made it up

BUT in 2008 he said:
"I was witness to an event
I cannot fully explain"


---


🔬 THE INVESTIGATION

Dr. John Mack — Harvard psychiatrist

Flew to Zimbabwe

Interviewed kids one by one

Asked them to draw what they saw

All drawings matched —
silver disc, big-eyed beings

Kids were crying, visibly shaken

62 children — same story — told independently

  STORY 3 — MICHAEL HERRERA
  INDONESIA 2009

🪖 WHO

Michael Herrera

20 years old

U.S. Marine rifleman

2nd Battalion, 5th Marines

6-man squad


---


🌊 WHY THEY WERE THERE

Sept 30, 2009

7.6 earthquake hits Sumatra

Triggered a tsunami

Marines sent for humanitarian aid

Stationed outside Padang, West Sumatra

Guarding aid supply drops


---


🌴 THE DISCOVERY

~1 week after earthquake

Squad patrolling in the jungle

No radios to report

Saw something massive in a clearing

Moved closer to investigate


---


🛸 THE CRAFT

300 feet wide

Octagonal shape

Vantablack — absorbs all light

Hovering silently below treetops

Rotating — changing colors

Low humming sound

Herrera started filming


---


🔫 THE AMBUSH

Got within 150 feet

8 men appeared

Black camouflage

Bulletproof vests

M4 rifles — night vision gear

American military jargon

Elite U.S. special ops

Drew weapons on the Marines


---


📦 WHAT THEY WERE LOADING

Modified Ford F-350 trucks nearby

Large weapons cases

Loaded onto platform under the craft

Platform rose up

Merged into the UFO


---


🤫 THE COVER-UP

Seized cameras

Memory cards + batteries gone

Took their guns and ammo

Air Force Lt. Colonel showed up

Forced to sign NDA + TSSI document

Threatened with treason

Capital crime — death or prison

🧠 THE BIGGER PICTURE

Goal: retrieve + reverse engineer

Consciousness-based tech

Worldwide race between nations

😰 THE AFTERMATH
